How to use the Pomodoro timer

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method Francesco Cirillo developed in the late 1980s with a tomato-shaped kitchen timer. The idea is simple: you split work into short, fixed intervals and take a break after each one. Use the timer like this:

  1. Pick one task. Not "study", but something concrete such as "20 physics problems from chapter 4".
  2. Start the 25-minute timer. Work only on that task until it ends; write any other thoughts on a piece of paper.
  3. Take a 5-minute break. Stand up, drink water, look out of the window. The break can start automatically (Settings → auto-start).
  4. Take a long break every four Pomodoros. After the fourth session, rest for 15 to 30 minutes; the timer suggests it for you.

Common mistakes and ways to adapt the method are in our guide What is the Pomodoro Technique?

What this timer does

  • Classic and custom lengths: 25/5/15 by default; focus from 1 to 180 minutes, breaks from 1 to 90.
  • Time left in the tab: While you work in another tab, the title shows something like "18:42 · Focus".
  • Chime and notifications: A short chime plays when time is up; allow notifications to get a desktop alert as well.
  • Background sound: Brown or pink noise generated by the browser itself, so you do not need a separate video or app. Our background sounds comparison explains which sound suits which task.
  • Privacy: No account and no cookies. Your settings and today's session count stay in your browser.
  • Accurate time: The timer tracks the end time instead of counting seconds, so it does not drift when the tab is in the background.

Which lengths should I use?

25/5 is a starting point, not a rule. These patterns are common depending on the kind of work:

PatternFocus / breakGood for
Classic Pomodoro25 min / 5 min, 15-30 min every 4 sessionsGetting started, practice questions, revision
Short cycle15 min / 3 minPeople who struggle to start or get distracted quickly
Long block50 min / 10 minDeep work such as coding, writing or learning new material
52/1752 min / 17 minLong days where you want longer breaks

Frequently asked questions

Is this online Pomodoro timer free?

Yes. The timer is completely free and needs no sign-up, ads or download. Your settings are stored only in your own browser.

Does the timer stop if I close the tab?

Yes, closing the tab stops the timer. As long as the tab stays open, time stays accurate even while you work in another tab. If you want a timer that keeps running when the app is closed, you can install Pomi on your phone.

Why is a Pomodoro 25 minutes?

Francesco Cirillo chose 25 minutes as long enough to make real progress and earn a break, yet short enough to keep full attention. It is not a scientific limit; if 15 or 50 minutes suits you better, change it in Settings.

After how many Pomodoros do you take a long break?

In the classic method you take a 15 to 30-minute long break after four Pomodoros. This timer uses 4 by default, and you can change it in Settings.

Is there a Pomodoro app for my phone?

Pomi is a free Pomodoro app for iPhone and Android. Every completed session grows a plant in your pixel garden, you can study with friends in live rooms and compete for your city on a conquest map. It has no ads.
