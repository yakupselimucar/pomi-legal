What is a virtual library, and why does it work?

A virtual library (or online study room) recreates the library feeling when you study alone at home: other people working at the same time, silence and fixed work blocks. YouTube "study with me" streams, Discord study servers and camera-on Zoom rooms are all versions of the same idea.

Two well-known effects explain why it works. The first is social facilitation: psychologists have long observed that we work more readily on simple, familiar tasks when others are around. The second is body doubling: someone quietly working next to you lowers the barrier to getting started, which people who struggle with procrastination and attention often describe. We gathered the details and research in our guide Study with me: why studying together works.

How studying together works in Pomi

  1. Create a room. Open a new room in Pomi; the app gives you a 6-character invite code.
  2. Share the code. Send it to friends, your class group or your team; anyone who enters it joins the room.
  3. Everyone starts their own session. There is no shared timer. When one person takes a break, nobody else's session is interrupted.
  4. See each other, work in silence. You can see who is working in the room; no camera or microphone needed. On breaks, you can message each other in the room chat.
  5. Make your effort visible. Every finished session grows a plant in your own pixel garden and scores points for your city on the conquest map.

Pomi rooms are opened with an invite code; there is no automatic matching with strangers. If you do not have a study group yet, start by sending the code to classmates. Even when you study alone, your sessions score points for your city on the conquest map, so you are still part of a team.

6 rules for productive online study sessions

  • Set the time in advance. Say "we are in the room at 8 pm" instead of "let's see tonight". A fixed time is as binding as walking to the library.
  • One task per session. Write down what you will do before you start; let your task, not the room, run the session.
  • Use your phone only for the timer. Turn off notifications; we listed ways to do that in How to stop checking your phone while studying.
  • Take breaks independently. With no shared timer, you rest when you need to and the others are still there when you return.
  • Save chat for breaks. A virtual library is valuable because it is quiet; use the room chat on breaks and mute it during a session.
  • Pair it with good study methods. A timer decides when you work; evidence-based study techniques decide how.

Can I study together from my computer?

Pomi rooms run in the iPhone and Android app. At your computer you can use the online Pomodoro timer on this site, but the web timer does not join a room or save your sessions. To appear in the room with your friends, open Pomi on your phone and keep working on your computer.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual library?

A virtual library is an online space where people study at the same time, aware of each other but without talking. YouTube study with me streams, Discord servers, video calls and rooms in apps like Pomi are used for this.

Is studying together in Pomi free?

Yes. Pomi is free to download, and creating a room to study with friends does not require payment. There are no ads; an optional Pomi Pro subscription is available.

Do I need to turn on my camera or microphone?

No. Pomi rooms do not need a camera or microphone; you see who is working in the room and everyone works quietly on their own timer. You can message each other in the room chat on breaks.

Is there a shared timer in Pomi rooms?

No. Everyone is in the same room but on their own Pomodoro timer. When someone takes a break or finishes a session, everyone else keeps going.

Can I study with strangers?

Pomi rooms are opened with a 6-character invite code; there is no automatic matching with strangers. Anyone you share the code with can join the room.

Can I join a Pomi room from my computer?

Pomi rooms run in the iPhone and Android app. On a computer you can use the online Pomodoro timer at pomifocus.com, but that timer does not connect to a room.
